## 2. Gaps in the original pages

- Terms lacked eligibility and authority, software licensing and business data rights, acceptable use, maintenance and third-party risks, suspension, termination, remedies and disputes. It described Professional as a live paid tier without explaining manual verification, expiry, or the difference between invoice settlements and plan payments.
- Tax language lacked customer review duties, GST-specific input and configuration risks, IRN/e-way bill limitations, credit-note handling, and a distinction between reporting exports and actual filing.
- Privacy omitted most categories and purposes, website and session technologies, vendors, international processing, rights, incident handling, access procedures and responsible contact details.
- Both pages lacked reliable business identity particulars, an approved effective date for revised terms, clear interlinks, and a browsable contents list.
- The previous eight-year claim treated different recordkeeping regimes as one rule. It also promised write protection, automatic deletion, and 30-day erasure that the code and archive blueprint do not substantiate.

## 3. Comparative research

- [Tally terms](https://tallysolutions.com/terms-of-use/), [privacy](https://tallysolutions.com/privacy-policy/), and [Auto Backup terms](https://tallysolutions.com/terms-and-condition-for-auto-backup/) distinguish product licence, connected services and backup obligations. Its desktop, partner, and renewal model should not be imported wholesale.
- [Zoho terms](https://www.zoho.com/terms.html), [privacy](https://www.zoho.com/privacy.html), [refund policy](https://www.zoho.com/refundpolicy.html), and [Zoho Books DPA guidance](https://www.zoho.com/qa/books/kb/gdpr/gdpr-dpa.html) separate organisation authority, trial data handling, recurring billing and contractual processor assurances. Bill Matrix has no corresponding recurring charge or executed DPA evidenced here.
- [myBillBook terms](https://mybillbook.in/web/terms) and [privacy](https://mybillbook.in/web/privacy-policy) identify the operator and distinguish products and ancillary processing. Its lending and other modules have no equivalent here.
- [Vyapar terms](https://vyaparapp.in/terms) and [privacy](https://vyaparapp.in/permission) show the importance of separate privacy and refund disclosures and distinguishing device permissions. Its app permissions and refund rules do not describe Bill Matrix.
- [BUSY terms](https://busy.in/terms-of-use/), [privacy](https://busy.in/privacy-policy/), and [licensing help](https://busy.in/faqs/license-management/installation-errors/1/) distinguish licensing, renewal, and support-assisted cancellation.
- [ClearTax terms](https://cleartax.in/meta/terms/index.html) and [privacy](https://cleartax.in/meta/privacy/index.html) distinguish GSTN dependencies, customer obligations, refund contact, and data handling. Bill Matrix does not offer ClearTax's filing or advisory services.

## 5. Publication decisions and operational follow-up

1. **Operator and grievances.** Confirm the footer name against incorporation records, registered office, CIN, any GSTIN/checkout supplier disclosures, and named grievance officer with contact channels. Repo verifies the configured support address, not the officer's appointment or registration. Confirm authority to use Bengaluru as a contractual forum before electing it.
2. **Payment remedies.** Approve refund/cancellation rules, deadlines, handling of rejected claims after receipt of funds, duplicate transfers and unused prepaid access. Confirm billing tax display, invoice/receipt issuance and escalation ownership. Review liability cap and narrow indemnity with Indian counsel before release. No unilateral no-refund rule or fixed cap assumed.
3. **Data lifecycle.** Reconcile the 30-day and blanket eight-year promises still visible in Settings with actual handling. Inventory production processors/regions, cross-border safeguards, backup expiry, support mailbox retention, evidence screenshots, log retention and legal holds. Implement and test closure, anonymisation where permitted, restoration, scheduled erasure, and proof of completion. Define a process that can receive lawful requests even from customers whose records were entered by a workspace.
4. **Acceptance and consent.** Record server-validated terms version and assent on both signup surfaces, with appropriate notice and distinct collection consent where required. The dedicated signup and hosted auth configuration need review. Confirm confirmation email, reset workflow and session/analytics cookies in production.
5. **Product claims.** Review the public auto-export badge, demo action labels, marketing estimates, and default invoice interest/returns text for accuracy. Test output against current CGST and income-tax formats, especially the ITR workbook's legacy 44AD naming after the 2025 Act transition. Do not confuse PDF archival with taxpayer recordkeeping, statutory e-invoicing, or certified WORM storage.
6. **Security and incident operations.** Verify vendor contracts and audit evidence, Indian log arrangements, reporting runbook, CERT-In contact, security review cadence, access review, escalation and tested backup restore. Scope applicable IT Rules duties with counsel. Recheck the DPDP timeline before release and before each commencement milestone.
7. **Policy placement.** Keep billing/cancellation, acceptable use and third-party disclosures in Terms, and cookies, rights, security and grievance information in Privacy. Once approved, show a concise refund summary and total price at checkout. Prepare a contractual DPA and subprocessor schedule for business customers when processor terms and vendor details are confirmed. Separate public pages are unnecessary for the current feature set unless a distinct checkout disclosure or cookie control becomes operationally useful.

## 6. Implementation and release status

- Replaced the existing public Terms and Privacy pages with linked, numbered documents. The shared legal layout provides anchored contents, readable line lengths and responsive spacing. Marketing header links now lead to homepage anchors from either legal route. No authentication, billing or data-handling runtime logic changed.
- Local Chrome verified both routes without sign-in, the 27 section targets, cross-links, footer links, header navigation, and layout at 320, 390, 768, 1,024 and 1,440 px.
- The pages are uncommitted review copies. The effective date is pending approval. The owner confirmed development status and directed use of explicit placeholders for operator, grievance contact, hosting, and retention details. The Terms include an expressly provisional refund and cancellation section based on the proposed rules. See [the launch checklist](LEGAL_LAUNCH_CHECKLIST.md) for outstanding decisions. Support is the verified repository contact and is not identified as the appointed officer. India remains the governing law, with competent forums determined by applicable law.
- The deployment runbook describes manual closure steps, a seven-day Neon PITR window, weekly logical backups and an eight-year Object Lock configuration. Those are documentation claims without independently verified production settings or completion evidence. Its universal eight-year statutory interpretation should be corrected. Validate its deletion SQL against referential and audit constraints before using it.
